**Google Business VoiceBox**
Voice-First Business Assistant for Indian MSMEs
Manage your inventory, billing, and marketing just by speaking!

Overview
Google Business VoiceBox is an AI-powered PWA designed to help small business owners in India manage their daily operations hands-free. Whether you run a Kirana store, a Restaurant, or a Boutique, simply talk to the app to get things done.

Now with full Hindi (हिंदी) Support! 🇮🇳

✨ Key Features
**Voice Assistant (English & Hindi)**
Hands-Free Control: "Add 50 packets of Milk", "Bill banao", "Check stock".
Language Toggle: Switch between English and Hindi with a single tap.
Smart Intent Parsing: Powered by Gemini AI, it understands natural language and Hinglish.
**Smart Inventory**
Quick Add: Add items instantly via voice or one-click suggestions.
Auto-Categorization: AI automatically categories items (e.g., "Coke" -> "Beverages").
Stock Alerts: Visual indicators for low stock items.
****AI Marketing Studio**
**Instant Designs: Generate professional HTML flyers/ads for your products.
Social Ready: Creates captions with hashtags for Instagram/WhatsApp.
Voice-to-Ad: Just say "Diwali sale ka ad banao" and get a ready-to-use design!
**🧾 Fast Billing**
Voice Invoicing: "Create bill for 2 Rice and 1 Oil".
Auto-Stock Deduction: Selling items automatically updates your inventory.
**🛠️ Tech Stack**
Frontend: React, TailwindCSS, Lucide Icons
Backend: Node.js, Express, Sequelize
AI Engine: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash
